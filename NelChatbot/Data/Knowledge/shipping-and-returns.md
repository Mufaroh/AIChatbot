# Shipping and returns

## Delivery
Delivery is free on all bikes within South Africa. Major metros (Johannesburg, Pretoria,
Cape Town, Durban) receive bikes in 2-4 working days; other areas take 5-8 working days.
Bikes arrive 95% assembled; you only need to attach the handlebar and pedals.
Accessories under R500 have a R95 delivery fee.

## Returns
You may return an unused bike within 14 days of delivery for a full refund.
The bike must have less than 20 km on the odometer and be in its original packaging.
Return collection costs R650, which is deducted from the refund.
Accessories can be returned within 30 days if unused.

## Refunds
Refunds are paid to the original payment method within 7 working days of the returned item being inspected.
