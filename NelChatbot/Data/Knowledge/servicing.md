# Servicing

## Free first service
Every new bike includes a free first service, to be done within 3 months or 300 km of purchase.
It covers gear and brake adjustment, bolt torque checks and a firmware update.

## Recommended schedule
After the first service, we recommend a standard service every 6 months or 1,500 km.
A standard service costs R850; a full service including drivetrain replacement labour costs R1,650.

## Battery care
Store batteries at 40-60% charge if the bike won't be used for more than two weeks.
Avoid charging below 0°C or above 40°C. Use only the original Nelisiwe charger.
