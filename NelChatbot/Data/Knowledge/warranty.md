# Warranty

## Frame warranty
All Nelisiwe frames carry a 5-year warranty against manufacturing defects for the original owner.

## Battery warranty
Batteries are covered for 2 years or 800 full charge cycles, whichever comes first.
If a battery holds less than 70% of its original capacity within that period, it is replaced free of charge.

## Motor and electronics
Motors, displays and controllers are covered for 2 years from the date of purchase.

## What is not covered
Normal wear parts (tyres, brake pads, chains, cassettes), damage from crashes or misuse,
water damage from submerging the bike or pressure washing, and unauthorised modifications
such as speed tuning. Tuning the motor voids the motor and battery warranty.

## How to claim
Bring the bike and proof of purchase to any Nelisiwe store, or email supportNelisiwebikes@NelCity.ac.za
with your order number and photos of the issue. Claims are assessed within 5 working days.
