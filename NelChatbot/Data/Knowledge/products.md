# Nelisiwe Bikes product range

Nelisiwe Bikes is a fictional e-bike retailer used as sample data for this chatbot.
Replace the files in Data/Knowledge with your own documents.

## Nelisiwe City
Our everyday commuter. 250W rear hub motor, 418Wh battery, range of 60-90 km.
Weight 21 kg. Integrated lights, rear rack and mudguards. Price: R24,999.
Available in sizes S, M and L, in Graphite and Sage Green.

## Nelisiwe Trail
A hardtail electric mountain bike. 250W mid-drive motor with 85Nm torque, 630Wh battery,
range of 50-110 km depending on terrain. 120mm front suspension, 29-inch wheels.
Weight 23.5 kg. Price: R42,500. Available in sizes M, L and XL.

## Nelisiwe Cargo
A long-tail cargo bike that carries up to 180 kg including the rider.
250W mid-drive motor, dual battery option (2 x 500Wh) for up to 160 km range.
Fits two child seats. Price: R54,900 (single battery) or R61,400 (dual battery).

## Choosing a size
Riders 150-165 cm usually suit size S, 165-178 cm size M, 178-190 cm size L,
and over 190 cm size XL. Test rides are free at all Nelisiwe stores.
